PDR Slide Deck description 

Objective:
The goal of this assignment is to prepare a comprehensive and professional slide deck for your Preliminary Design Review (PDR) presentation. This presentation will demonstrate your project’s progress, your understanding of the problem, and your design approach to date. Review both the rubric and the guidelines stated in the assignment prompt.

Instructions:

Teams MUST submit their slide deck to Carmen and to their sponsor the night before their presentation to receive full credit. 

Presentation Content:

Title Slide: Include the project title, team name, and members' names. Add the date of the presentation.
Project Overview: Provide a brief introduction to the project, including the problem statement, objectives, and the significance of the project.
Problem Identification: Summarize the problem you are addressing. Include the background, scope, and any key insights gained from your problem identification process.
Needs Assessment: Present the identified needs of the stakeholders. Highlight the major needs and how you’ve prioritized them.
Requirements Definition: Outline the key functional and non-functional requirements of your project. Include any constraints and assumptions. These usually appear in the form of a table that maps needs to the project requirements.
Design Approach: Describe your design approach and methodology. Include initial design concepts, sketches, or diagrams as applicable. Presenting a block diagram of the overall system can help the audience understand your design approach better. 
Progress to Date: Show the current status of your project. Include completed tasks, milestones achieved, and any prototypes or models developed.
Challenges and Risks: Discuss any challenges or risks you have encountered and how you plan to address them.
Next Steps: Outline the upcoming tasks and milestones. Include a timeline or Gantt chart to show the planned progress.
Questions and Feedback: Prepare to address questions from the reviewers. Include a slide inviting feedback and questions from the audience.
Slide Deck Requirements:

Professional Design: Ensure your slide deck is visually appealing and professionally designed. Use consistent fonts, colors, and layouts. Avoid clutter and ensure that each slide is focused on a single topic or idea.
Clarity and Brevity: Each slide should be clear and to the point. Use bullet points, diagrams, and visuals to convey information effectively. Avoid excessive text and focus on key points.
Visual Aids: Use charts, graphs, diagrams, and images to support your presentation. Ensure that all visual aids are labeled clearly and relevant to the content.
Rehearsal: Practice your presentation to ensure smooth delivery. Be prepared to discuss each slide in detail and answer questions from the audience.
Submission Requirements:

Deliverable: Submit the final slide deck through Carmen. Ensure that all team members’ names are included on the title slide.
Format: The slide deck should be formatted professionally, with a clear and logical flow of information. Use high-quality images and ensure that all text is readable.
Evaluation Criteria:

Review the Rubric attached to this assignment while considering the following:

Content Quality: Accuracy and completeness of the information presented.

Presentation Skills: Clarity of communication, engagement with the audience, and ability to answer questions effectively.
Design and Organization: Visual appeal, clarity, and logical flow of the slide deck.
Adherence to Guidelines: Following the specified structure and requirements.
Purpose of this Exercise:

This assignment will help you develop and refine your presentation skills, which are crucial for effectively communicating your project progress and plans. The PDR presentation is an opportunity to showcase your work, receive valuable feedback, and demonstrate your readiness for the next stages of your project.
